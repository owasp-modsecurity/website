---
title: "What is the difference between ModSecurity and CRS?"
weight: 10
noindex: true
---

The key difference is that ModSecurity is the Web Application Firewall (WAF) engine, while the OWASP Core Rule Set (CRS) is a collection of rules that runs on top of it. ModSecurity handles request inspection and enforcement, and CRS provides patterns for detecting common web attacks. You can use ModSecurity with CRS or with other custom or third-party rule sets, depending on your needs.
