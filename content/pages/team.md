The landmark [transfer of ModSecurity custodianship to OWASP](https://owasp.org/projects/modsecurity) promises to inject fresh energy and perspectives into this project. OWASP’s vast network of security experts and volunteers can now directly contribute to the project’s core. This powerful WAF is poised to further solidify its position as a cornerstone of web application security, protecting countless websites against the ever-evolving threat landscape.

## Interested in Contributing?

Interested in joining the ModSecurity development effort? We welcome contributions in many forms – code, reviews, documentation and testing.

[Get in touch with the team](mailto:modsecurity@owasp.org) or [head over to Slack](https://owasp.slack.com/archives/CBKGH8A5P) to see how to get involved.
