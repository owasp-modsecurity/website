## A New Chapter for ModSecurity

Trustwave has transferred custodianship of ModSecurity to the Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP). This transition marks a new chapter for the project, ensuring that **the engine remains actively maintained, community-driven, and aligned with the evolving needs of modern web application security**.

Under OWASP’s stewardship, **ModSecurity benefits from broader collaboration, tighter integration with the OWASP Core Rule Set (CRS), and a renewed focus on openness and long-term sustainability**. The project is very much alive, and with the collective energy of maintainers, contributors, and users, we are moving forward together to support the next generation of WAF protection.
